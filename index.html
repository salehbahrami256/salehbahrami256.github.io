About

I started in chemical engineering — a field built on dynamical systems, reaction networks, and the stubborn fact that most interesting behavior comes from parts interacting, not parts in isolation. That instinct followed me into computer science: an MSc at Sharif University, where I worked on clustering algorithms for high-velocity data streams under Dr. Alireza Zarei, and finished as one of the top-ranked students in the program.

Two years in industry as a risk data scientist in retail finance sharpened a suspicion I already had: a lot of the phenomena I care about — how risk propagates, how communities form, how systems drift — are relational before they are tabular. A row in a spreadsheet is usually a node that's been stripped of its edges. That's the problem I want to spend a PhD on.

Outside of research, I TA'd algorithmic game theory, analysis of algorithms, data structures, and convex optimization at Sharif, and I review for ECAI. I'm not attached to any one subfield for its own sake — I care about problems with real mathematical depth and a real system behind them, not a benchmark to chase.

Research

My central question: how do you model complex systems — biological, financial, physical — using the mathematics that actually fits their structure, rather than forcing them into vectors? Network science is the throughline; everything else feeds into it.

Network science & complex systems Topological data analysis Graph neural networks & geometric deep learning Neural algorithmic reasoning Causal & multi-agent modeling Streaming & large-scale data

Concretely, I spend a lot of time with simplicial complexes and persistent homology as a way to describe higher-order structure in networks — relationships that involve more than two nodes at once — and I'm increasingly drawn to work on algorithmic alignment and length generalization in learned models, in the spirit of what Petar Veličković's group works on. The applications I actually care about are in biology, medicine, and the natural sciences; I'm far less interested in advertising or engagement-style networks.

Currently: finishing a paper on adaptive multi-resolution grid clustering for data streams — a reservoir-based extension of my MSc work — with a target of an arXiv preprint this fall. Code is public as it develops.

Projects

Adaptive multi-resolution grid clustering for data streams

A hierarchical, memory-bounded clustering method for high-velocity data streams — extends the D-Stream family with adaptive-precision grids, time-decayed per-cell statistics, and pruning under a fixed memory budget. Originally my MSc thesis; now being rebuilt with a proper merge step and benchmarked against fixed-resolution baselines ahead of an arXiv submission.
