About Me

Hi! I’m Saleh, a computer scientist passionate about algorithms, data, and network science. I enjoy exploring the intersection of theory and real-world applications.

Research Interests:
I am interested in the mathematical foundations of artificial intelligence and complex systems, with a particular focus on network science, geometric methods, and statistical learning. My goal is to explore interdisciplinary approaches that combine ideas from geometry, topology, graph theory, optimization, and machine learning to study the structure and dynamics of real-world networks. I am especially curious about geometry-inspired descriptions of networks, including Ricci curvature, manifold-based perspectives, and topological representations of relational systems. I am interested in understanding how such mathematical structures can contribute to deeper theoretical insights in representation learning, graph machine learning, and the analysis of large-scale complex data. I also enjoy applying statistical inference and machine learning methods to practical problems involving social, financial, and biological networks.
